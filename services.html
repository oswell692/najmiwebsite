Customs Clearance & Bond Services

Customs Clearance

Our office aims at clearing consignments within the stipulated Zambia Revenue Authority clearance period. Road importations and pre-clearances are arranged to reduce delays at the border.

Clearing Bulk Goods

Specialized handling and clearance of bulk goods with expertise in managing large volume consignments efficiently.

Removals in Bond (R.I.T/R.I.B)

We are licensed with a Customs Bond to remove goods in bond and we have the capacity to move huge volumes as well as increase bonds as per clients' request.

Tracking of Consignments

Najmi International works to ensure that no penalty and demurrage charges are incurred, provided that our clients and their suppliers send us pre-alerts of shipments to track consignments. Our presence at all borders enables us to timely attend to all clearance of consignments.

Transportation

We outsource a large fleet of trucks that transport goods 24/7 from all Eastern, Central and Southern African Countries.

Warehousing

Najmi has Customs and Excise Licensed Transit Area which provides storage for goods pending the completion of customs formalities. Customs cleared goods are also stored while awaiting final delivery to clients. Our storage facility is secured and has enforced security features.

Storage & Delivery

Warehouse & Truck Parking

We have warehouses and truck parking facilities in Lusaka to provide our clients with storage for all consignments as they await final delivery.

Final Delivery

We ensure timely and safe final delivery of all consignments to our clients' doorsteps.
