Who We Are

Najmi International Ltd is a licensed Shipping, Freight Forwarding, and Customs Clearing Agent that was established in 2001. It has since become a reliable logistics solutions provider that caters to the needs of its various clients.

We are registered members of the International Federation of Freight Forwarders Association (FIATA) and the Zambia Customs and Forwarding Agent Association (ZCFAA), ensuring that we meet international standards and best practices in the logistics industry.
